# Look targets

The renderings in `../plan/renderings/` are the visual target for the Windows build: from the same
camera, our frame should be hard to tell from the rendering, apart from geometry that the plan has
since changed. `Scripts/lookmatch.ps1` renders each view below and puts it beside its rendering.

Valid targets: 01, 02, 03, 04-v2, 05-v2, and 07 for mood. 06 (Hall of Light) and 08 (the Sphere) show
superseded designs: take their mood only.

## Everywhere
- **Photographic, not CG:** soft, deep bounced light, gentle contrast, no clipped whites (skies,
  skylights and glass stay readable), no haze or bloom halos, crisp contact shadows.
- **Stone:** pale honed travertine and marble in long ashlar courses, fine joints, gentle tonal
  variation, no visible repeat.
- **Floors:** large polished travertine or marble slabs with clear, soft reflections of the walls,
  art and light.
- **Gilt:** frames are carved and gilded, reading as gold, never flat yellow.
- **Glass:** clear, low-iron, with a faint teal edge.

## 01 · Arrival, Salon Impression (`01-salon-arrival.png`)
- **Camera:** the east end of Bay 1, just inside the Rotunda passage, looking west down the axis;
  eye 1.6 m; about 24 mm (74° horizontal); level.
- **Light:**
  - Soft daylight from the crown skylights, with warm sun patches on the floor.
  - Discreet warm picture lights over each frame.
  - A warm cove wash along the vault springing.
- **Elements:**
  - Impression, Sunrise on its glass stele, right of centre, 3 m away.
  - Four transverse arches in one-point perspective.
  - The wall colours in order: oxblood, grey, sage, rose, ochre.
  - A coffered barrel vault with a narrow crown skylight.
  - A glowing arched far door.
  - Flush round viewing stones on the axis.

## 02 · Bay 2, Modern Paris (`02-modern-paris.png`)
- **Camera:** from the south-west corner of Bay 2 looking north-east; eye 1.6 m; about 24 mm.
- **Light:**
  - An overcast rainy afternoon through the skylights, with no direct sun shafts.
  - Warm picture lights on the three paintings.
  - A warm wash at the vault springing.
- **Elements:**
  - Grey silk wall with a fine weave: Caillebotte centred, a Pissarro either side.
  - The Little Dancer on a pale stone plinth, right foreground.
  - A monumental travertine transverse arch, the sage bay beyond.
  - A polished floor with reflections.

## 03 · The Nymphéas oval (`03-nympheas-oval.png`)
- **Camera:** near the west door looking east across the oval; eye 1.6 m; about 20 mm.
- **Light:**
  - A luminous fabric velarium overhead with soft, dappled leaf shadows.
  - Even, diffuse, cool-warm daylight.
- **Elements:**
  - Lime-white plaster walls.
  - The Water Lilies panels unframed, 2 m tall, continuous round the walls.
  - The oval pond: a travertine kerb, dark still water, pads and pink-white lilies.
  - Four curved backless travertine benches, each one continuous piece.
  - A travertine floor.

## 04 · The Reserve (`04-the-reserve-v2.png`)
- **Camera:** in the central aisle looking east; eye 1.6 m.
- **Light:**
  - Dim and archival.
  - Warm globe pendants in pools.
  - Thin teal lights along the rack tracks.
  - Spot pools on the easel and the pulled-out rack.
- **Elements:**
  - Warm red brick groin vaults on square brick piers with stone bases.
  - Brass-mesh racks edge-on, one pulled out with three gilt-framed Renoirs.
  - Wooden plan chests.
  - A dark polished concrete floor.

## 05 · The Rotunda (`05-the-rotunda-v2.png`)
- **Camera:** eye 1.6 m at the west portal, looking east, tilted up 24°; 18 mm (about 90° horizontal).
- **Light:**
  - Natural sun only, through the eye, making a crisp lattice-patterned pool on the floor near hour
    IV.
  - Broad soft bounced daylight everywhere else; no artificial uplights.
- **Elements:**
  - A pale marble drum with fluted pilasters, bases and capitals, and a 150 mm shadow gap.
  - Four arched portals with archivolts, four empty niches.
  - A deep coffered hemispherical dome, 5 rings × 28.
  - A glazed triangulated oculus with a bronze node.
  - The bronze sun clock inlaid in polished marble; the motto HORAS NON NUMERO NISI SERENAS.

## 07 · The Atrium (`07-future-atrium.png`, mood)
- **Camera:** tilted up about 20°, looking across the hall at the car (the plan's door is west).
- **Light:**
  - Frosted misty glass glowing soft white-grey, with no sky showing.
  - A soft even floor.
  - A warm cove line at the base of the wall.
  - The glass car glowing warm inside.
- **Elements:**
  - Twelve white ribs.
  - A cylindrical all-glass car with slim bronze rings, doors facing the camera.
  - A polished pale stone floor.
  - The Starry Night on its glass stele by the car.
