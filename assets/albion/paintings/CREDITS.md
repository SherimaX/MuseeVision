# Albion image credits

Images for the Albion wing (Victorian England, 1848–1898). Downloaded 2026-09-25 for a personal, non-commercial prototype. Paintings are cropped to the painted surface and saved as JPEG (quality 92) with the long side capped at 8192 px; untouched downloads are kept in `../_originals/`. Full per-image data (catalogue size, sight shape, pixel size, crop and download notes) is in `SOURCES.json`.

Every work shown is in the public domain: all the artists and makers died more than 100 years ago. Under PD-Art, faithful reproductions of 2D public-domain works carry no new copyright. The Met, Cleveland and Birmingham photographs are released as CC0. No image here needs attribution.

**Shapes.** *Ophelia*, *The Awakening Conscience* and *The Long Engagement* have arched (round-topped) sights; *An English Autumn Afternoon* and *The Last of England* have oval sights. Their images keep the whole canvas or panel, so the bare corners must be masked by the frame.

**Resolution.** Wikimedia rate-limited downloads of original files on the day, so many Commons images are the 3840 px standard thumbnails rather than the larger originals (see `SOURCES.json`); several Tate pictures (*King Cophetua*, *The Golden Stairs*, *Proserpine*) and *The Stonebreaker* exist only as small public-domain scans.
