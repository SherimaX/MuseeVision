# Painting image credits

Images for the 37 works hung in Salon Impression (44 pieces: the five Rouen Cathedrals and the four Water Lilies panels count separately; Degas's *Little Dancer* is a sculpture and has no image yet). Downloaded 2026-09-23 for a personal, non-commercial prototype, and resized to about 2048 px on the long side (the four Orangerie panoramas are 4095 px wide). Every painting is in the public domain: all the artists died more than 100 years ago. Under PD-Art, faithful reproductions of 2D public-domain works carry no new copyright.

**Attribution required** for two gallery photographs that are licensed, not public domain: *Flood at Port-Marly* (CC BY 3.0, Sailko) and Rouen Cathedral 3, *Harmonie brune* (CC BY-SA 4.0, Gargarapalvin). Credit these two if the app is ever shared.
