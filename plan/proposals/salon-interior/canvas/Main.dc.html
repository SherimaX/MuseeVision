Salon Impression

the same line, a new interior

The building stays as built: the straight 84-metre line from the Rotunda to the Water Lilies, five bays under one coffered vault, a lantern in each crown. Only the inside changes. In the Unreal build, piers, arches, vault and floor are one pale stone in one even light; the chapter colours hide behind the piers, and the paintings are the smallest things in view.

1

Colour on the piers

Each bay’s silk wraps onto the faces of its piers, corner to corner. From the door you read the chapters in order down the line. Jambs, arches and vault stay stone: the bones frame the colour.

2

Oak to stand on, stone to pass

Versailles parquet in every bay, travertine under every arch. The floor counts the bays along the line and warms the room.

3

Five lanterns, five lights

Same lanterns, new glass: clear, opal, leaves, muslin, clear. Each chapter gets the daylight it was painted in, taken live from Giverny.

4

Benches beside the line

A pair of velvet benches in each of bays 2 to 5, in the bay’s own colour, 2.6 m either side of the axis and just behind the viewing stones. The line stays clear to walk.

5

The 1874 case

Opposite the stele, a table case with the first exhibition’s catalogue and Louis Leroy’s review in Le Charivari, the article that named the movement.

6

The oval closes the ring

The Orangerie’s second cycle, 51 m of willows, runs round with joints under a metre. The pond is built as planned: a low oval of black water.

7

Half-light above

The vault drops into half-light so the paintings are the brightest thing you see. A gilt rosette in each coffer, voussoirs and a keystone on every arch.
