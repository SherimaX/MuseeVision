SALON IMPRESSION · INTERIOR UPDATE · LIGHT

Five lanterns, five lights

light is the subject

The lanterns keep their size and place in the crown of each bay; only the glass changes, one chapter each, so walking west you pass from one kind of daylight to the next. All of them take their sky from Giverny, live: sun or cloud over Monet’s garden now, at your hour. The Rotunda keeps the hour and Albion the weather; the Salon keeps the light.

BAY 1 · CLEAR GLASS

Plein air

The bare sun, as the painters took it outdoors. A sharp patch crosses the oak through the day.

BAY 2 · OPAL GLASS

Paris under cloud

Milk glass spreads the light evenly: the flat grey of Caillebotte’s rain and the station roof.

BAY 3 · UNDER LEAVES

The dance under the trees

A canopy planted over the lantern. The sun falls through moving leaves onto you, as it falls on the dancers at the Moulin de la Galette.

BAY 4 · MUSLIN

A curtained room

A linen blind in the lantern: the soft, shadowless light of the rooms where Morisot and Cassatt painted.

BAY 5 · CLEAR, MOVING

Light that changes

The series was about the hour. High in June, low in December, the patch of sun walks across the bay towards the Rouen row.

GIVERNY, LIVE · THE OVAL UNDER FOUR SKIES

Monet wanted the Water Lilies seen by daylight from above, never by lamps. The velarium over the oval takes the sky over his pond as it is now, so the panels change as the real water does. Sun position stays at your hour, as everywhere in the museum; cloud, rain and haze come from Giverny.
