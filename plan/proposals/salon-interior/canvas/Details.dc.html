Details

silk, stone, oak, gilt, velvet

The architecture keeps its geometry and gains its finish. Silks one step deeper, so gilt frames and broken colour glow against them. Stone that shows it is built: voussoirs, a keystone, coursed jambs. A gilt rosette in every coffer to catch the lantern light. Velvet, oak and brass where hands and feet touch.
