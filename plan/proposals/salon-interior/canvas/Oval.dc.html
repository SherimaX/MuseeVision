SALON IMPRESSION · INTERIOR UPDATE · THE OVAL

The Nymphéas oval

the end of the line

The end of the line should be the richest room, and in the build it is the barest. At their true sizes the four panels of the Orangerie’s first room come to 40 m, and the oval’s wall to 55.4 m, so more than 15 m of it stands bare between them. The four panels of the Orangerie’s second room, the willows, come to 51 m and run round with joints under a metre: the painting wraps you, as Monet meant.

Built as planned

An oval of 8.4 × 4.6 m that echoes the room, not a round basin. Kerb 0.3 m, honed travertine; the water dyed black so it becomes a mirror. Lilies in clumps, as at Giverny.

The first room’s panels

Morning, Clouds, Green Reflections and Setting Sun leave the hang. The day cycle is lovely, but at true size it cannot close the ring here.
