SALON IMPRESSION · INTERIOR UPDATE · PLAN

The plan, furnished

nothing structural moves

The plan is the Salon as built; nothing structural moves. Drawn here is what goes inside: the silk that now wraps each bay’s pier faces (the coloured ticks at every arch), oak parquet in the bays with travertine under the arches, a pair of benches either side of the line in bays 2 to 5, the 1874 case beside the stele, the willow cycle round the oval and the pond as it was planned.

WALKING BACK · FROM THE OVAL DOOR, LOOKING EAST

The ladder in reverse

Every pier has two faces, and each takes the colour of the bay it looks into. Walking west you meet oxblood, zinc, sage, old rose and haystack, with the white oval glowing at the end; walking back the same arches read haystack, old rose, sage, zinc, and the oxblood end wall with the Rotunda’s door open in it. The stele shows its back: linen and stretcher behind the glass.
