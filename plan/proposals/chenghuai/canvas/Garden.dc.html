GARDEN PLAN · NORTH UP
THE GARDEN · 臥遊

Wòyóu, travelling at rest

宛自天開

宗炳, too old to climb, painted his mountains on the wall and travelled them lying down. Here it goes the other way: after the paintings, you walk them. The garden stands beside the house, never inside its formal court, as 可园 does beside its scholar’s house. At the moon gate the materials change from Beijing red and grey brick to Suzhou white walls and chestnut timber.

PRECEDENT · 可园, 帽儿胡同 (1861)

漏窗 · LATTICE WINDOWS WHERE THE GALLERY WALL MEETS THE COURTS

石兄

A 3 m Taihu rock stands 3 m inside the moon gate, framed by the circle as you step through. Mi Fu bowed to such a rock and called it elder brother.

林泉

The flower hall is furnished, not hung: a painting table set for work, where scrolls were unrolled among friends. Guo Xi: look with the heart of forests and streams.

遊目

Twelve 书条石 in the gallery wall, cut after the Chunhua Pavilion model letters. Wang Xizhi’s phrase closes the loop the Lanting opened.
