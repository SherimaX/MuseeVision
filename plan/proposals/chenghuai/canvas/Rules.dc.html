天棚 鱼缸 石榴树

A reed awning in summer, a fish bowl, pomegranates in tubs, and crabapple before the main hall. The old Beijing saying lists them.

屏门 closed

The 垂花门’s inner screen stays shut, and everyday traffic goes round by the galleries. It opens only for weddings, funerals and festivals.

The garden beside

Gardens copied from Suzhou (可园, 谐趣园, 静心斋) sit beside or behind the house, never in its formal court.
