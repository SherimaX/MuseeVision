SECTION · ELEVATIONS

Height ranks the house

前低後高

The 正房 stands highest on the tallest plinth. Next come the gate and the side halls; the front and rear rows are lowest. The same order shows in the depth of the eaves, the number of steps and the painting on the beams.

举架

Each roof steepens toward the ridge: 5, 7 and 9 tenths of each step. That gives the curve.

苏式彩画

Beams painted in the Suzhou manner, with a 包袱 scene at each bay: Suzhou already inside Beijing.

硬山 · 卷棚

Brick gables on the halls; the side halls and galleries have rounded, ridgeless roofs.
