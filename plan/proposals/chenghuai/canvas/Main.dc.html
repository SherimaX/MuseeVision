Chenghuai

澄懷 · 臥遊

A proper three-court Beijing house on the Rotunda’s north door, with a Suzhou garden beside it. Both names come from 宗炳, the first writer on landscape painting: 澄懷觀道，卧以游之, “clear the heart to see the Way, and travel through it lying down.” The house holds the pictures; the garden is where you walk them.

Rooms for the art

Writing in the north-lit front row, ceramics in the side halls, the Song landscapes in the main hall, the long scrolls in the rear row. Light, height and case follow the works.

One door

The Rotunda stands where the lane would be. Its north door, the porch and the 广亮大门 share one axis. There is no other way in or out: the garden brings you back to the gate.

The garden beside

The inner court stays formal. The Suzhou garden runs the full depth to the east: pond and hall at the north, rockery and pavilion at the south.
