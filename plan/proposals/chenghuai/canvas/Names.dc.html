The names of the house

題名

Sixteen inscriptions: plaques, a couplet, a carved panel, a rock. Each is drawn as it would be made, with the line it comes from.

澄懷觀道
影壁 · the screen wall inside the gate
Chénghuái guān dào · clear the heart, and see the Way
老疾俱至，名山恐難遍睹，唯當澄懷觀道，臥以遊之。
《宋書 · 宗炳傳》: Zong Bing (375–443), too old to climb, painted his mountains on the walls and travelled them lying down
臨池
倒座房 · calligraphy
Línchí · by the ink pond
臨池學書，池水盡墨。
衛恒《四體書勢》, on Zhang Zhi, who practised by a pond until its water turned black. 臨池 has meant “to practise calligraphy” ever since

天青

Tiānqīng · the blue of the sky after rain

the order said to have been given to the potters by Emperor Shizong of Later Zhou: the colour the Ru basin reaches
昌南
东厢房 · porcelain, Yuan to Qing
Chāngnán · the old name of Jingdezhen
Jingdezhen was called 新平, then 昌南, until the Song emperor Jingde gave it his reign name. Every piece in the room was fired there

行到水窮處，坐看雲起時。

清閟
西耳房 · Ni Zan, Wang Meng
Qīngbì · pure and secluded
清閟閣, Ni Zan’s library pavilion at Wuxi, where he kept his books, bronzes and paintings
停雲
东耳房 · Shen Zhou, Wen Zhengming
Tíngyún · the clouds that linger
停雲，思親友也。
陶淵明《停雲》: Wen Zhengming named his studio 停雲館 after the poem. Suzhou painters, remembering friends
舒卷
后罩房 · the two handscrolls
Shūjuǎn · unrolling, rolling up
what one does with a handscroll, an arm’s length at a time; what clouds do. The room is read that way, walking west
臥遊
月洞门 · into the garden
Wòyóu · travelling at rest
唯當澄懷觀道，臥以遊之。
宗炳, the second half of the line on the screen wall. The name of the garden
石兄
峰石 · the standing rock
Shíxiōng · elder brother rock
芾見大喜曰：此足以當吾拜！具衣冠拜之，呼之為兄。
《宋史 · 米芾傳》. Mi Fu, whose Sichuan Silk lies in the first room, bowed to a rock and called it brother
林泉
花厅 · the studio
Línquán · forests and streams
以林泉之心臨之則價高，以驕侈之目臨之則價低。
郭熙《林泉高致》: look at a landscape with the heart of forests and streams. Guo Xi’s Early Spring hangs in the hall
知魚
水榭 · the water pavilion
Zhīyú · knowing the fish
子非魚，安知魚之樂？
《莊子 · 秋水》. As at 寄畅园’s 知鱼槛, the garden Qianlong copied as 谐趣园
見山
亭 · the rockery pavilion
Jiànshān · seeing the mountain
採菊東籬下，悠然見南山。
陶淵明《飲酒》. From the pavilion you look south, and the southern mountain is the Rotunda’s dome: a borrowed view (借景)
遊目
复廊 · the gallery of engraved stones
Yóumù · letting the eye roam
所以遊目騁懷，足以極視聽之娛。
王羲之《蘭亭集序》. The last words you pass are the first text of the house
HOW THE NAMES ARE MADE

A literati house names itself; it is not labelled. Every name here is a phrase with a source, chosen for the thing it names: the ink pond of the calligraphers, the blue of Ru ware, the studios of Ni Zan and Wen Zhengming. Most come from the few texts the house is about: 宗炳 on travelling through painted mountains, 郭熙 on how to look at them, 王羲之’s Lanting. The English only glosses the Chinese.

Inscriptions are cut and gilded in traditional characters, as in a Qing house. Horizontal plaques read right to left; couplets hang with the first line on the right. Building types (正房, 厢房) stay in simplified characters on the drawings, as in a restoration survey. The rear court is left without a name.

The gate carries no words. By the Qing rule for officials below duke and marquis (柱用素油，门用黑饰), its leaves are black lacquer, with a pair of brass knockers (门钹), four plain door pins (门簪) and the carved stone 门墩: rank is read from the depth of the door, not from writing on it.
