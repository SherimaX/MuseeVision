PLAN · THE VISIT

One door, one loop

曲徑通幽

The Rotunda door is the only way in or out. The route is ordered by the art: writing first, then clay and fire, then the great landscapes, the small rooms of the recluses, porcelain, the long scrolls, and last the garden, which brings you back to the gate.

LIGHT FOLLOWS THE ART

Silk and paper go where no direct sun reaches: the north-lit front row, the deep hall, the paper-glazed rear row, the small side rooms. Ceramics, which can take light, get the east- and west-facing side halls.

LEVELS AND NEIGHBOURS

Stairs throughout, as in a real house: five steps up to the gate (0.9 m), two to the 垂花门, three to the 正房, and two in each gallery. The Sculpture Hall moves to the Rotunda’s south door. The south-west corner clears the Manet cabinet by 0.8 m.
