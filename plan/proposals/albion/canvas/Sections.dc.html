One storey

No galleries, no clerestory, no stone vaults: the arcade carries iron straight into glass, as at Oxford. It reads as a museum court, not a church.

Two screens

Both ends of the nave are iron and glass above 8.5 m. The north one is clear; the south one carries the Tristram and Isolde band from 9.0 to 12.6 m.

The noon sun

At noon at the equinox the London sun stands 38.5° up and lays the band’s colours on the nave floor 11 to 16 m from the south wall.

The eye line

From the gilt sun, 1.6 m up, the eye runs 53 m to the middle of the tapestry at 2.2 m. The porch and door frame it; nothing stands in the way.
