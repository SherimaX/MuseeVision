ALBION · 04 · THE HANG

The hang

Twenty-nine places, hung two deep

Small canvases hung as the Victorians hung them: two deep around a line 1.6 metres up, so the bays read full. The west aisle is truth to nature, 1848–68; the south end is legend; the east aisle is beauty, 1860–96. Drawn at true size.
