The Rotunda

A coffered dome 20 m across, just under half the Pantheon, with a steel-and-glass eye. You arrive on the gilt sun at its centre; four doors lead out, and four casts stand in the niches. Raise the bronze sun clock and a stair opens under it, down to the Classical Hall.

The floor keeps the hour

A 14 m bronze sundial in the tradition of Rome's meridian lines. A bronze node in the lattice casts a point of shadow that walks the numerals from VI to VI.

The one touch of future

The sun keeps your local time on an even path, 15° an hour in every season, so the point always lands on the hour.

A lattice in the eye

A regular steel-and-glass grid in the spirit of the British Museum's Great Court, 20.3 m up. Its bronze node throws the point of shadow that keeps the hour.

The floor lifts

From the ring, take the prompt Raise the sun clock. The dial rises 3.5 m in 8 s on a marble drum, and a stair opens under it. It never lowers while anyone is inside or below.

The Classical Hall

Raise the sun clock and the dial becomes a small temple with a door to the west. Inside, a spiral stair goes 6 m down to a landing, and a round-arched port opens north into a barrel-vaulted sculpture gallery and a round tribune, after the Vatican's Braccio Nuovo and Sala delle Muse. The casts are from SMK's Royal Cast Collection, public-domain 3D scans.

SECTION · ON THE AXIS, LOOKING EAST · NORTH TO THE LEFT
Inside the raised drum: the coffered soffit under the dial and the bronze balustrade where the stair turns down.
The gallery: paired giallo antico columns, casts in Pompeian-red niches, busts on consoles, a laylight in each bay of the coffered vault.
The tribune: pavonazzetto columns under a coffered dome with gilt rosettes; the Apollo Belvedere and the Laocoön in the niches, the Belvedere Torso in the middle.

The stair

40 risers of 150 mm, with a landing halfway, round an open well 4.4 m across. Twelve spots hidden in the drum's coffers light the treads; daylight comes in through the drum's door.

The gallery

6 m wide and 24 m long, in four bays. Ionic columns of giallo antico on a rosso antico dado, a coffered barrel vault (crown 5.15 m), a floor of porphyry discs in giallo squares, after the Pantheon's.

The tribune

13.2 m across, after the Vatican's Sala delle Muse: an ambulatory round a ring of twelve pavonazzetto columns that carries a coffered dome with a laylit eye. Five niches and ten busts in its wall.

Casts and light

41 casts stand here, 45 with the Rotunda's four: statues, busts on consoles and herms, reclining figures on the axis. The laylights give a steady electric daylight (it is underground). All light 3800 K.
