SECTION II · ÉLAN · LEVEL −1 · EARTH

The Square

Under the round Atrium lies a 28-metre square of rammed earth: the ground to its sky. It is divided into nine squares and numbered by the Lo Shu. The elevator lands on 5, the centre. Around it are four open galleries and four dark rooms, all kept for new art.

NINE SQUARES · 洛书 LO SHU

Every row, column and diagonal adds up to 15. Five, the number of earth, sits at the centre, and that is where the car stops. The numbers are cast in bronze in each floor.

SECTION · LOOKING NORTH, THROUGH THE CAR

Round sky, square earth

A ring of light in the ceiling traces the Atrium’s circle overhead, so the circle and the square are always in view together.

Rammed earth

Walls of rammed earth, 1.2 m thick, laid in visible layers. The sound goes quiet and dry.

The only daylight

The real sun comes down the glass shaft onto 5. Look down through the glass floor there: soil, clay, chalk, bedrock.
