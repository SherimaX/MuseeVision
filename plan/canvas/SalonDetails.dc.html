ROOM 01 · DESIGN DEVELOPMENT · SHEET 3 OF 4 · DETAILS

Details

Classical bones throughout: a coffered barrel vault on transverse arches, a cornice, fabric walls. The modern and future touches are in the details: the shadow gap, a light-grid skylight, a glass stele, stones and placards that answer your gaze.

TRANSVERSE SECTION THROUGH BAY 2 · LOOKING WEST
THE NYMPHÉAS OVAL · SECTION · LOOKING WEST
Lower and softer than the bays. Panels hang 0.7 m above the floor so the water lilies sit at the level of your gaze, and daylight arrives through a fabric velarium. At the centre, a shallow pond of living water lilies, ringed by four curved stone benches that face the panels.
