SOUTH WING · CLASSICAL

The Chinese Wing

四時園

Through a moon gate off the Rotunda: a whitewashed cloister on three sides of an open garden, the Garden of the Four Seasons, after the courtyards of Suzhou and the Met's Astor Court. The Rotunda keeps the hour; this garden keeps the season.

The garden keeps the season

The one touch of future. The open court follows the 24 solar terms and your local time: plum at Lichun, lotus at Xiazhi, snow on the rock at Daxue.

The moon in the pond

At night the real moon, on its real phase, clears the south wall. From the terrace you see it lying in the pond, as the gardens of Suzhou intended.

A scroll is read, not hung

The handscroll opens an arm's length at a time, right to left, rolling on as you walk north beside it.
