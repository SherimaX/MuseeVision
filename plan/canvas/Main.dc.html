Musée Vision

Classical bones. Modern light. Future glass.

A museum you step into. Every visit begins under a domed rotunda, on a bronze sun clock. Its doors open onto Impressionist painting, sculpture and a Chinese garden; eastward, each room keeps its classical proportions while taking one more step toward modern light and future glass.

I · CLASSICAL

The bones

Proportion, symmetry and procession. Marble floors, coffered vaults, pilasters, and doorways that line up into one long view.

II · MODERN · ALIVE

The light

Restraint around the art, life around the room. Shadow gaps instead of mouldings, walls of glass, and gardens growing right up to them.

III · FUTURE

The glass

What only a virtual building can do. Parametric lattices, placards that float, thresholds that glow, and rooms at impossible scale.
