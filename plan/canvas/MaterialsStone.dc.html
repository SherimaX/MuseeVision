THE MATERIAL LANGUAGE · I

One stone, finished three ways

The museum is built of four families that run through every room: one stone, one white, one bronze, one oak. Each era changes the finish, never the material. Honed becomes polished becomes glass-smooth, and every doorway carries the same detail: the floor stone runs through, with a brushed-bronze strip at the line. The Rotunda is the anchor. Every swatch is the texture the engine uses, at the material's own colour.
