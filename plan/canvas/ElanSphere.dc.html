SECTION II · ÉLAN · LEVEL 2 · SKY

The Sphere

A sphere 28 m across sits in the Atrium like a ball in a cup: its equator on the drum at 22 m, its underside the Atrium's ceiling. The glass car climbs through a pearl neck, sealed by an iris at each end, to a stone platform at the exact centre, the only point where an image on a sphere looks true in every direction. Inside, the building is gone: the live sky is all round you, below your feet too.

The sky above you, now
The real sky for the museum's city at this moment: the stars of the Yale Bright Star Catalogue, the Moon and the Milky Way. There is no sun inside, so by day it shows the stars the sun hides.

The sky becomes The Starry Night
The live sky slowly turns into Van Gogh’s brushwork, keeping the promise of the painting beside the car.

Kind to the eyes

It starts and stops softly, then runs at a steady 1.5 m/s. The frosted glass floor and the glass rail stay with you the whole way.

A sky that holds still

The stars are drawn at infinity, so they do not slide past as you rise.

Down again

Step out and send the car down: it sinks into the neck and the iris shuts flush, a full stone floor. Call it back to ride down.

IN GAME · THE PLATFORM, CAR SENT DOWN
The car has gone down: the platform's iris has shut over its way, flush with the pale stone, the seven blades a spiral of fine lines.
IN GAME · FROM OUTSIDE
From outside: a travertine plinth, the misty glass drum framed by twelve pearl fins, a stone entablature at the equator, and the Sphere's upper half as a lattice dome under a lantern.
