SECTION II · ÉLAN · LEVEL 2 · SKY

The Sphere

Boullée’s 150-metre shell, used the way Las Vegas uses its Sphere: the whole inside is one image. The glass car climbs from the Atrium, through the south pole, to the exact centre, the only point where an image on a sphere looks true in every direction. It is the real sky above you by default, and new art when a show is on.
