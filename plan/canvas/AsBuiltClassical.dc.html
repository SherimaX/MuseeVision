AS BUILT · I · CLASSICAL

The Rotunda and the rooms below it

The Rotunda keeps its sun clock and four doors, and now its floor leads down as well. Raise the bronze sun clock and a stair opens under it to the Classical Hall: 45 casts of the antique, set among marble and gilt.

MATERIALS

Photographic stone

Every stone is photographed, not painted: honed limestone, travertine, giallo antico and pavonazzetto. Each block takes its own part of the photograph, so no two neighbours repeat.

THE CASTS

45 works, scanned

Plaster casts in the Royal Cast Collection (SMK, the National Gallery of Denmark), scanned in 3D and in the public domain. Each is set upright at true size, faced and labelled.
