THE MATERIAL LANGUAGE · II

The white, the bronze, the oak

Round the stone: the lime whites overhead, one bronze alloy in every fitting, one oak for everything the hand touches. Glass and water complete the set. All of it is physically based, so ray-traced light tells each material from the next.
