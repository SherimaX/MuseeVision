AS BUILT · LEVEL −1 · THE RESERVE

The Reserve, rebuilt

Under the whole Salon, a brick undercroft: a nave with an aisle each side, 60 m long and 24 m wide, under groin vaults that rise to 5.6 m. The racks slide out into the nave, and any painting on a rack can go to the easel at the east end and back. It replaces the low cellar on the earlier Reserve sheet.

PLAN · LEVEL −1 · NORTH UP · 1 M = 30 PX
STRUCTURE

Nave and aisles

14 brick piers 0.70 m square, chamfered, on travertine bases 0.54 m high, with imposts at 4.0 m. Eight bays of 7.5 m. Pier lines at y ±5.0 m, 10 m apart centre to centre.

STORAGE

36 racks, 8 plan chests

Racks 5.0 × 3.6 m: brass frames round brass wire mesh on bronze carriages, running in flush floor tracks. One out at a time, gliding 5.65 m into the nave. Four islands of two walnut plan chests in bays 7–8; the Degas pastels lie on felt on the south islands.

LIGHT

Globes and uplights

An opal globe in every bay: 44 cm and 3400 lm in the nave, 34 cm and 2600 lm in the aisles, all 3800 K. Four shadowless uplights per pier wash the vaults.

THE WORKS

13 unhung works

Eleven paintings on racks A–E (F waits for new ones) and two Degas pastels on the chests. Rack C starts out in the nave, and Le Pont de l'Europe on the easel.
