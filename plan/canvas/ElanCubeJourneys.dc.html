SECTION II · ÉLAN · LEVEL −1 · THE CUBE · WHAT IS ON

Two journeys

Before the car goes down you choose one, the mountain or the sea, on two bronze signs on its rail. Each is one day, about twelve minutes, told in six places. The car never moves: the world is rebuilt round it. Within a place the view may drift at walking pace, always level, never turning; between places a cloud, or a shoal of fish, fills the Cube and clears on the next.

EACH SCENE LOOKS NORTH FROM THE CAR
INSIDE THE DASHED SQUARE: IN THE ROOM, NEAR
OUTSIDE IT: BEYOND THE WALLS, FAR
I
THE MATTERHORN · 4,478 M · SWITZERLAND AND ITALY

A day on the mountain

Chosen for its shape: one clean pyramid of rock and snow, standing alone. The museum already knows it. Its first photograph was a daguerreotype made for John Ruskin on the Riffelsee in 1849, in the years of the Hall of Light’s first prints, and Edward Whymper’s party first climbed it in 1865. The journey follows the Hörnli route, from the lake to the summit, from dawn to night.

DAWN TO NIGHT · SIX PLACES · ABOUT 12 MIN
BETWEEN PLACES A CLOUD ROLLS IN AND CLEARS
1
DAWN · RIFFELSEE · 2,757 M
The mountain in the lake

The Matterhorn stands in the Riffelsee, its summit turning pink before the rest. The lake lies just under the car, so the reflection is at your feet. The first photograph of the mountain was made on this shore, for John Ruskin, on 8 August 1849.

HEAR · WIND ON THE WATER, COWBELLS FAR BELOW
2
MORNING · THEODUL GLACIER · C. 3,000 M
Inside the glacier

The cloud clears inside a crevasse: blue ice a few metres either side, a slit of sky above, blue dark below. The room is full of ice you could almost touch.

HEAR · THE ICE CREAKS BESIDE YOU; MELTWATER DRIPS
3
LATE MORNING · HÖRNLI HUT · 3,260 M
The foot of the ridge

Beside the hut where the climb begins, the Hörnli ridge rises 1,200 m above you. Look up and find the rope teams strung along it.

HEAR · BOOTS ON SCREE, VOICES ON THE TERRACE, A STONE FALLING FAR OFF
4
AFTERNOON · SOLVAY HUT · 4,003 M
On the ridge

You hang beside the little emergency hut on the crest. The east face falls away under your feet to the glacier, spindrift blows past, and a fixed rope runs within reach.

HEAR · WIND, AND THE ROPE TAPPING THE ROCK
5
SUNSET · THE SUMMIT · 4,478 M
Above the clouds

The summit snow lies just under the car. Peaks stand out of a sea of cloud: Monte Rosa to the east, the Weisshorn to the north, Mont Blanc far to the west where the sun goes down. Whymper’s party stood here first, on 14 July 1865.

HEAR · ALMOST NOTHING: WIND ACROSS THE CREST
6
NIGHT · THE SUMMIT · 4,478 M
Under the stars

Night comes up from the valley: the Milky Way overhead, Zermatt’s lights 2,870 m below. Then it snows inside the Cube, the snow thickens to white, and the car rises to the Sphere and the real sky.

HEAR · SNOW, THEN SILENCE
II
RAJA AMPAT · WEST PAPUA, INDONESIA

A day under the sea

The richest reefs on earth, in the heart of the Coral Triangle: more kinds of coral and fish than anywhere else, and sharks found nowhere else. The journey goes from the surface to the edge of the deep, from morning to night, and ends where it began.

MORNING TO NIGHT · SIX PLACES · ABOUT 12 MIN
BETWEEN PLACES A SHOAL OF FISH CLOSES IN AND CLEARS
1
MORNING · PIAYNEMO · THE SURFACE
Half in, half out

The sea’s surface crosses the Cube at eye level: limestone islands and sky above, the reef below. Look down and you are under water; look up and you are not.

HEAR · LAPPING ABOVE, THE REEF’S CRACKLE BELOW
2
MORNING · THE MANGROVES · 0–3 M
Among the roots

Mangrove roots come down all round you, with corals growing on them, in one of the few places the two live together. Young blacktip reef sharks slip between the roots.

HEAR · SNAPPING SHRIMP, RAIN ON THE SURFACE
3
NOON · CAPE KRI · 5–25 M
The richest reef

In the current off Kri Island a shoal of jacks closes into a ring round the car and turns. On one dive here in 2012 Gerry Allen counted 374 species of fish, still the record.

HEAR · THE CURRENT, AND THE SHOAL TURNING
4
AFTERNOON · MANTA SANDY · C. 15 M
Mantas

Reef mantas, 3–4 m across, circle a coral mound where small wrasse clean them. They pass over the car, under it and beside it; one glides so low you see its gill slits.

HEAR · THE REEF’S HUM, AND WINGBEATS YOU FEEL MORE THAN HEAR
5
LATE AFTERNOON · THE WALL · 30–40 M
Into the blue

A wall of sea fans drops into the deep blue. On the fan beside you sits a pygmy seahorse 2 cm long, close enough to see its eye; far out in the blue, a column of barracuda.

HEAR · DEEP QUIET
6
NIGHT · THE SURFACE
The sea’s own stars

Back at the waterline after dark: stars over the islands, and below, the plankton flashing blue wherever the water moves. On the reef flat a walking shark, found only here, walks on its fins. Then the lights go out one by one.

HEAR · NIGHT BIRDS ON THE ISLANDS, THE SEA BREATHING
