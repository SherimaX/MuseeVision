Sculpture Hall

One top-lit court, 16.8 m square inside, north of the Rotunda. It stands empty for now. Its ten scanned sculptures were not good enough to show, so they were taken out, with their plinths and lights, until better ones are found.

PLAN · ONE COURT, EMPTY FOR NOW
SECTION · LOOKING NORTH

Empty for now

The ten scans, Rodin and his time, were taken out because they were not good enough. Their plinths and accent lights went with them. The court waits for better ones.

Light only from above

A hipped glass roof over a laylight, 14 × 14.4 m. The diffuser stops the sun, so daylight reaches the court only as the laylight's glow.

Travertine all round

Vein-cut ashlar walls 0.6 m thick, an 18 cm skirting, a plaster cornice. The polished floor runs on through the door and the passage to the sun clock's edge.

AS BUILT · THE COURT TODAY

The works are removed for now

The court was planned for ten sculptures from open 3D scans, from Rude to Bourdelle with Rodin at the heart. In the game they did not look good enough, so all ten were taken out, with their plinths and their lights. Only the travertine bench on the west side is left. New works will come in only when better scans are found.

The room itself is finished: the walls, the laylight, the glass roof and the door from the Rotunda are as drawn above.

TAKEN OUT

La Marseillaise (head), The Dance, The Age of Bronze, The Gates of Hell, The Thinker, The Kiss, The Burghers of Calais, Monument to Balzac, The Walking Man, Hercules the Archer.

SCULPTURE IS DOWNSTAIRS

The Classical Hall runs under this court, 6 m down, with 41 plaster casts of Greek and Roman sculpture. The way in is the stair under the Rotunda's sun clock: see the Rotunda board.
