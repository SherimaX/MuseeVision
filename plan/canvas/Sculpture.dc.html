NORTH WING · CLASSICAL

Sculpture Hall

One top-lit court, 17 m square, north of the Rotunda. Ten works from Rude to Bourdelle, 1833–1909, with Rodin at the heart. Every one comes from an open 3D scan.

PLAN · ONE COURT, TEN WORKS
SECTION · LOOKING NORTH TO THE GATES

One axis, one destination

From the sun rosette you look through the north door straight at The Gates of Hell, 30 m away. Nothing stands on the axis.

The Gates' two children

The Thinker and The Kiss both began as figures on the Gates. They stand either side of it, back beside where they started.

No pedestal for the Burghers

The six burghers stand on the floor, as Rodin wanted, so you walk in among them at their own height.
