ROOM 01 · CLASSICAL

Salon Impression

An 84-metre enfilade of five skylit bays, one chapter of Impressionism each, with its own wall colour. A side cabinet for Manet, and at the end an oval of Monet's Water Lilies around a pond of living lilies. You enter from the Rotunda facing Impression, Sunrise, with the whole axis opening beside it.
