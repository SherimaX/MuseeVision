ROOM 01 · CLASSICAL

Salon Impression

An 84-metre enfilade of five bays under a coffered vault, a lantern of glass in each crown, one chapter of Impressionism each, with its own silk wall colour. A side cabinet for Manet, and at the end an oval of Monet's Water Lilies around a pond of living lilies. You enter from the Rotunda facing Impression, Sunrise, with the whole axis opening beside it. The pond lifts to open the stair down to the Reserve.
