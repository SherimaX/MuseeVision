THE VISIT · A WINDOWS GAME IN UNREAL ENGINE 5.8

From the gilt sun to the stars

You walk the museum on a Windows PC, with a keyboard and mouse or a gamepad. Every visit begins on the gilt sun in the Rotunda, facing the west door. The same museum streams to Vision Pro in VR.

One way round

The doors, the stairs and the car set the order. The Reserve and the Classical Hall are dead ends: you come back up the way you went down.

01ROTUNDA · START

The Rotunda

You arrive on the gilt sun at the centre of the sun clock, facing the west door. Four casts stand in the niches.

02WEST

Salon Impression

Five colour bays under glass lanterns. Impression, Sunrise waits on a glass stele in Bay 1; the Manet cabinet opens off it.

03NYMPHÉAS OVAL

Lift the pond

Use the golden lily and a line of light runs round the rim. Use it again: the pond rises 2.6 m on four bronze posts.

04LEVEL −1

The Reserve

36 steps down to a brick undercroft. Pull out a rack, then send one of its paintings to the easel at the east end, and back.

05ROTUNDA

Raise the sun clock

Step off the dial and press “Raise the sun clock”. It rises 3.5 m in 8 s, and inside a spiral stair of 40 steps goes down 6 m.

06LEVEL −1

The Classical Hall

A coffered gallery of casts opens into a round tribune on twelve columns: 41 casts from SMK's Royal Cast Collection.

07NORTH

The Sculpture Hall

A top-lit court of travertine, empty for now. Its scans were taken out until better ones are found.

08SOUTH

The Chinese Wing

The stele in the vestibule shows today's solar term. Through the moon gate: the garden, the cloister, scrolls and ceramics.

09EAST

The Hall of Light

Glass between a birch meadow and an orchard. Early photographs bay by bay, and four stereo stones.

10ÉLAN · LEVEL 0

The Atrium

Stand on the bronze ring and call the car. The Starry Night stands on a glass stele beside it.

11ÉLAN · LEVEL −1

The Square

The first ride goes down to the earth: the Lo Shu's nine squares, and a glass floor over a pit 7 m deep.

12ÉLAN · LEVEL 2

The Sphere

Then up at 1.5 m/s, through a neck sealed by irises, to the centre: the live sky all round, below your feet too.

THE VR BUILD · MUSEEVISIONVR

On Vision Pro, through SteamVR and ALVR

The same museum, rendered on the PC and streamed to the headset. No Mac is involved. Without a headset, the same exe is the desktop game.

USE

Look at it, then pinch the right thumb and index finger.

STEP

Pinch and hold the right hand. A gilt ring shows where you will land; let go to step there.

WALK

Pinch and hold the left hand to walk where you look.

TURN 30°

Thumb to middle finger: the right hand turns right, the left hand turns left.

ANOTHER HOUR

Left thumb to ring finger.

RECENTRE

Left thumb to little finger: eyes back to 1.6 m, facing ahead.

Your eyes are set at 1.6 m, sitting or standing. The buttons float low on your right; wall labels come up low on your left. Walking round your room moves you through the museum; walls stop your body, not your head. Not yet tried in a real headset.

House rules

01
Every visit begins on the gilt sun, facing the west door.
02
Nothing skips. The car rides at 1.5 m/s, and its first ride goes down before it goes up.
03
The sun clock never lifts you, and never lowers while you are inside it or below.
04
The sky is live. The sun clock keeps the local time of Beijing or New York.
