From your living room to the Rotunda

The museum never drops you in cold. It arrives as a doorway in your own room, and you decide how far to step in. Once inside, you stand still and the building comes to you.

STEP 1

Arrive

A small window floats in your own room. Tap Enter.

STEP 2

A doorway opens

The Rotunda appears as an arched portal in your wall, sunlight and all.

STEP 3

Turn the Digital Crown

The museum grows around you until your room is gone. Turn back to return at any time.

STEP 4

Look, pinch, glide

Look at a glowing viewing stone and pinch. The museum glides to you; you never move.

STEP 5

Meet the painting

Pinch a painting to open its glass placard. Pinch again to hear its story.

Moving through the museum

WALK

Take a few real steps. The floor is true to scale for about a metre and a half around you; step further and the museum gently fades to show your room.

GLIDE

Viewing stones sit where a curator would want you to stand. Look at one and pinch: the museum slides to you with a soft vignette, and you arrive facing the art.

JUMP

Look straight down and a bronze compass appears at your feet. Pinch it to open the museum map and go to any room, or back to the Rotunda, where every visit begins.

Comfort rules

01
Nothing moves unless you pinch.
02
You always arrive facing something worth seeing.
03
Horizons stay level: no turning, no bobbing.
04
Light leads. The next place to go is the brightest thing in view.
