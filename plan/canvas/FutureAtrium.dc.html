SECTION II · ÉLAN · LEVEL 0 · FUTURE

The Atrium

Section II looks forward. A 28-metre circle of twelve bays under misty glass: one door from the Hall of Light, eleven bays kept for new art, and at the centre a glass elevator that joins the square earth below to the stars above. Beside its door hangs one painting, The Starry Night.

BY THE ELEVATOR · THE ONE PAINTING
The Starry Night
Vincent van Gogh, 1889 · Museum of Modern Art, New York · 74 × 92 cm

A small square of night in a daylit room, on a glass stele beside the car door, 1.9 m off the axis. It is the sign for the elevator: this car goes up to the stars.

ELEVEN BAYS FOR NEW ART

Élan is for new art: our own commissions, planned later. Every bay is the same, so any work can go in any bay. Bay F, on the axis, is seen from the Hall of Light through the glass car and is kept for the headline piece.

Misty glass overhead

The real sun, at your local time, comes through as a soft, even haze. The Sphere stays unseen until you arrive.

Classical base, future glass

Travertine up to 6 m where the art hangs; above it only ribs and misty glass, leaning in to a ring at 22 m.

The wait is part of the visit

No skip. Wait on the bronze ring beside The Starry Night as the car glows down through the misty glass. Down to the earth first, then up to the stars.
