SECTION II · ÉLAN · LEVEL 0 · FUTURE

The Atrium

Section II looks forward. A 28-metre drum of twelve bays: travertine to 6 m, misty glass to 22 m, and overhead the glowing underside of the Sphere, carried on twelve pearl ribs. One door from the Hall of Light, eleven bays kept for new art, and at the centre a round glass car that joins the square earth below to the stars above. Beside its door stands one painting, The Starry Night.

BY THE ELEVATOR · THE ONE PAINTING
The Starry Night
Vincent van Gogh, 1889 · Museum of Modern Art, New York · 73.7 × 92.1 cm

A small square of night in a daylit room. It hangs in a slim gilt frame on a glass stele 1.5 m wide and 2.2 m high, in a brass foot, 1.9 m north of the axis by the car's door. It is the sign for the elevator: this car goes up to the stars.

ELEVEN BAYS FOR NEW ART

Élan is for new art: our own commissions, planned later. The stone pilasters are gone, so every bay is the same and any work can go in any bay. Bay F lies on the axis, seen from the Hall of Light through the glass car.

HALF SECTION · TRUE SCALE
ROUND SKY, SQUARE EARTH · 天圆地方

A glowing ceiling

The Sphere's underside is the ceiling: glass on a triangulated grid of pearl-white steel, like the British Museum's Great Court. Two rings of twelve lights under it follow the daylight.

Twelve pearl ribs

One chamfered section, 0.42 by 0.30 m, all the way: from a bronze shoe on the floor, plumb up the wall, round a 3 m arc at 9 m and under the Sphere to the ring. A fine bronze line runs up each face.

The wait is part of the visit

Call the car from the bronze ring. The iris in the collar opens and the car glows down out of the neck: about 13 s. Down to the earth first, then up to the stars.

IN GAME · THE STELE
The painting on its stele, the glass tube behind it. The ribs rise from bronze shoes along the wall.
IN GAME · FROM BEHIND
From behind, through the glass: the canvas's own back, unbleached linen on a pine stretcher with its keys. The west door is beyond.
THE IRIS · SEVEN BLADES · TO SCALE
Seven curved blades, each a 110° arc 1.25 m wide, turn 52° on their pins in 4 s and shut like a camera's diaphragm. One seals the collar over the Atrium, one the neck's mouth in the Sphere, one the platform. See As built · Élan.
