AS BUILT · WINDOWS · UNREAL ENGINE 5.8 · SEPTEMBER 2026

The museum, as built

Every room of the plan now stands in Unreal, walkable at 4K. The concept holds: classical bones, modern light, future glass. These boards show the building as it is today, photographed in the game.

THE FAÇADE

A front for the whole

A stone front wraps the rooms: the portico, a balustraded attic and round-headed windows. The domes of the Rotunda and the Élan rise behind it, as they do in plan.

THE GROUNDS

Lawn up to the walls

Real mown lawn all round. It is cut back wherever the building opens into the ground: round the Rotunda's drum, the Élan shaft and the lily pond's stair, so nothing grows over a way down.

THE SKY

The real sky, at your hour

The sun and moon at your local time and place. At night the stars and the Milky Way (NASA's all-sky photograph) turn overhead, and the Sphere shows them from its centre.

THE VISIT

Walk, stride, hop

Walk at 2.6 m/s, hold Shift to stride at twice that, and press Space for a low hop. Hardware ray tracing (Lumen) lights every room. DLSS 4.5 adds upscaling, ray reconstruction and frame generation.
