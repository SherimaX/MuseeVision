AS BUILT · THE CHINESE WING · 四時園

The Garden of Four Seasons

Through the moon gate, a Suzhou courtyard: a Taihu rock, bamboo, osmanthus and plum, and a covered walk all round. The lettering is cut the way a garden cuts it, in clerical script (隸書), gilt on stone and on dark lacquer.

THE SOLAR-TERM STELE

Today's term, in stone

The stele in the vestibule shows the solar term of the day you visit, worked out from the sun's longitude: one of 24, in two large gilt characters and nothing else. The font lacks 蟄, so it is set from its own 執 over 虫, the way the character is built.
