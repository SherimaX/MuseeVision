MASTER PLAN · AS BUILT · LEVEL 0 AND −1

One building, three eras

Every visit starts on the gilt sun under the Rotunda's dome, with the Classical Hall below it. West lies the Salon over the Reserve; north, the Sculpture Hall; south, the Chinese Wing. East, the Hall of Light leads to Élan: the Atrium, the Sphere above and the Square below. The front faces south over a long pool.

The route from the Rotunda

STARTCLASSICAL

The Rotunda

Where every visit begins: a bronze sun clock under a lattice eye, four doors, and four casts in the niches.

LEVEL −1CLASSICAL

The Classical Hall

Raise the sun clock: a spiral stair goes down 6 m to a vaulted gallery and a round tribune. 41 casts from SMK.

WESTCLASSICAL

Salon Impression

Five colour-coded bays, the Manet cabinet, and the Nymphéas oval round a living lily pond.

LEVEL −1HIDDEN

The Reserve

A brick undercroft 60 × 24 m, wider than the Salon over it. 36 racks glide out into the nave; an easel at the east end.

NORTHCLASSICAL

Sculpture Hall

A top-lit travertine court, empty for now: its ten scans were taken out until better ones are found.

SOUTHCLASSICAL

Chinese Wing

Through a moon gate: a garden court and a cloister, ceramics, scrolls, and a stele with today's solar term.

EASTMODERN · ALIVE

Hall of Light

28 m of glass between a birch meadow and an orchard, hung with the first photographs.

LEVEL 0ÉLAN

The Atrium

Misty glass and twelve pearl ribs. The Starry Night on a glass stele, and a glass car on the axis.

LEVEL −1ÉLAN

The Square

The Lo Shu's nine squares in rammed earth. The car lands on 5, over a glass floor and a 7 m pit.

LEVEL 2ÉLAN · FINALE

The Sphere

Ø 28 m, sitting on the drum like a ball in a cup. From its centre, the live sky all round.

SOUTHOUTSIDE

The front

Six Ionic columns with MVSÉE · VISION on the frieze, over a 30 m pool, lamp standards and box parterre.

ALL ROUNDOUTSIDE

The grounds
