One building, three eras

Every visit starts under the Rotunda's dome. West lies the Salon and its Water Lilies; north, sculpture; south, the Chinese Wing. East, the Hall of Light leads to Section II, Élan: the Atrium, the Sphere above it and a square floor below.

One top-lit court: Rodin and his time, with The Gates of Hell on the axis.

SOUTHCLASSICAL

Chinese Wing

Through a moon gate: handscrolls, ink landscapes, a courtyard garden.

EASTMODERN · ALIVE

Hall of Light

30 m of glass between two gardens, hung with the first photographs.
