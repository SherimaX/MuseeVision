MODERN · ALIVE · EAST OF THE ROTUNDA

Hall of Light

A glass hall 30 metres long and 9 wide, from the Rotunda to the Atrium between two gardens. Glass from floor to roof: the Rotunda's steel-and-glass lattice, drawn out into a shallow vault. The first photographs hang on the glass, with the garden growing behind them.

Modern, and alive

Outside the glass everything grows: blossom in spring, seed heads in winter, grass moving in the wind. Rain drums on the lattice, as in the Rotunda.

Prints on the glass

One print per 3 m bay, at eye level on a slim bronze-edged panel, with garden above, below and between. Here daylight cannot fade them.

A clear axis

The four stereo stones, each with an 1850s stereograph in 3D, stand off the centre. The view runs clear to the glass car and The Starry Night.
