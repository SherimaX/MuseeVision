ROOM 01 · SHEET 1 OF 4 · PLAN

Salon Impression, expanded

The Salon grows from one room into an 84-metre enfilade: five bays under a coffered vault, each lit by a glass lantern in its crown and hung in its own silk colour and chapter of the story, a side cabinet for Manet, and an oval where Monet's Water Lilies wrap around you. You enter from the Rotunda facing Impression, Sunrise, the painting that named the movement, with the whole axis opening beside it.
