The lily pond and the Reserve

There is no door. The whole lily pond at the centre of the Nymphéas Oval lifts into the air, water and flowers and all, and beneath it a long stair runs down east, under the oval floor, into the Reserve: a brick undercroft 60 metres long and 24 wide under the entire Salon. A nave runs between two arcades of brick piers under groin vaults, and in the aisles the works not on the walls wait on sliding brass racks.

RACK C · PULLED OUT · WORKS AT TRUE SCALE
Each rack is a brass frame 5.0 m long and 3.6 m high round a wire mesh, on a dark bronze carriage over two floor tracks. Use a rack and it glides 5.65 m out into the nave; one is out at a time. Use a painting on it and it is carried to the easel at the east end; use it there and it goes back. Through the mesh you see each painting's own back: linen, pine stretcher, keys.
HOW IT IS BUILT
ROOM
A nave and two aisles, 60 × 24 m inside, eight bays of 7.5 m. It lies under the whole Salon and the lawn either side; its top is 5 cm under the Salon floor.
PIERS
Brick, 0.7 m square with chamfered edges, seven a side on lines 10 m apart. Travertine bases, and travertine imposts at 4.0 m, where every arch springs.
VAULTS
Brick groin vaults over all 24 bays. Nave crown 5.61 m, aisles 5.31 m. Brick arch bands run across and along the arcades.
FLOOR
Dark polished concrete at −5.8 m, with slim bronze tracks set flush under the racks.
LIGHT
An opal globe on a bronze rod in every bay, 3800 K, larger in the nave. Four small uplights on each pier's impost wash the vaults. One spot lights the easel.
STAIR
36 travertine steps of 161 × 339 mm in a brick shaft 2.4 m wide. Bronze handrails 0.9 m up, a line of light under each. It arrives through an arched door, 2.4 m wide, on the axis.
