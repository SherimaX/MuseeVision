The lily pond and the Reserve

There is no door. The whole lily pond at the centre of the Nymphéas Oval lifts into the air, water and flowers and all, and beneath it a long stair runs down to the east, under the Oval floor, into the Reserve: a 60-metre brick-vaulted cellar beneath the entire Salon, where every work not on the walls waits on sliding racks.
