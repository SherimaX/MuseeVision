# GPT renderings

Concept images of eight views, made with an AI image generator from the board compositions on
2026-09-23 (1536 × 1024). Each `.md` beside an image holds its exact prompt and review notes. They are
mood targets, not the plan: where an image and a board disagree, the board wins. The artworks and
inscriptions in them are AI-redrawn.
